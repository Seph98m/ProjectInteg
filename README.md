## 📝 Overview

The Orthodocs Dental Clinic Management System addresses the need for streamlined administration and record-keeping in dental practices. It replaces outdated manual methods and inefficient filing systems with a digital, centralized, and user-friendly solution.

Common problems such as misplaced patient histories, scheduling issues, and poor data handling prompted the development of this system. Orthodocs ensures dependable and organized workflows that allow clinics to efficiently manage vital information.

Built as a database-driven system, Orthodocs helps clinics achieve better patient care, reduced administrative workloads, and efficient management of patient records. It also improves data integrity, enhances security, and ensures high availability of data.

With Orthodocs, clinics can deliver services in a more professional and responsive manner. The system marks a significant step toward digital transformation in the health sector, demonstrating how technology can enhance not only individual health outcomes but also the practices that support healthier populations. It supports long-term, sustainable growth and enables more accessible healthcare services in the future.

## ▶️ Usage

### 🧑‍⚕️ Patient Interface

#### A. Logging In
1. Open the Dental Clinic Management System on your browser or desktop.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

#### B. Registering and Viewing Appointments
- Click **Schedule a Meeting**.
- Choose the **type of service**.
- Select a **date and time** from the available schedule.
- Add a **message or notes** describing the symptoms.
- Click **Submit** to finalize the appointment.

---

### 👩‍💼 Admin Interface

#### A. Logging In
1. Open the Dental Clinic Management System on your browser or desktop.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

#### B. Appointment Scheduling
- Go to the **Appointments** section.
- Click on an existing **Appointment**.
- View details of the patient who booked the appointment.

#### C. Managing Patient Records
- Navigate to the **Patients** section.
- Select and view the patient who booked the appointment.

#### D. Billing and Payments
1. Go to the **Billing** tab.
2. Select a **patient** and input **treatment details**.
3. Choose a **payment method**.
4. Enter the **amount**.
5. The system will **auto-generate** the bill.
